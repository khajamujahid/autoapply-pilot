How {{ APP_NAME }} works

Self-hosted. Your data and keys stay in ./data on your machine.

 Browser ──► FastAPI web app (auth, pages, JSON)          ./data/app.db (SQLite or Postgres)
                 │                                          ./data/uploads   resumes
                 ▼                                          ./data/artifacts tailored PDFs + screenshots
          Worker (in-process thread or separate container)
   ┌──────────── pipeline per application ───────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ QUEUED → fetch JD + detect ATS → match score → OPTIMIZING_RESUME (PDF)      │
   │        → OPTIMIZING_CL → REVIEW ──you approve / auto-approve≥X──► APPROVED  │
   │        → APPLYING (Playwright) → APPLIED | DRY_RUN_DONE | NEEDS_MANUAL | FAILED │
   └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   Scheduler: saved searches every N hours → Discover (Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby boards, Remotive, Arbeitnow)
   AI layer:  one Provider (Grok · Gemini · Claude · OpenAI · custom · mock) for parsing, tailoring,
              cover letters, question answers and match explanations.

Answers

Form questions are answered from your saved profile answers first (work authorization, sponsorship, EEO = decline…), and only unknown questions go to your AI.

Safety

Dry run on by default. Review step on by default. Daily limit per user. CAPTCHAs are never bypassed: the job becomes NEEDS MANUAL with a link.

Privacy

Each user only sees their own data. AI keys and site passwords are Fernet-encrypted with the key in .env / data/.encryption_key.
